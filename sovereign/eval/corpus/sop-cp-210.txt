plant-alpha|sop-cp-210|internal|SOP CP-210 Coolant Pump P-7 Vibration Limits
Synthetic document for demonstration. The vibration limit for coolant pump P-7 is 7.1 millimetres per second RMS. Readings between 4.5 and 7.1 millimetres per second require a follow-up inspection within one week. Readings above 7.1 millimetres per second require immediate shutdown and escalation to the shift supervisor.
