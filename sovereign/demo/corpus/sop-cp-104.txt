Synthetic document for demonstration. Coolant pump P-7 must be inspected every 500 operating hours. The inspection checks bearing temperature, seal leakage and vibration. Bearing temperature above 85 degrees Celsius requires the pump to be taken out of service. Seal leakage above 20 drops per minute requires seal replacement within 48 hours. Before any inspection the engineer must review the sensor alarm log for pump P-7.
