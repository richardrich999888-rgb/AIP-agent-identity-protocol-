# P0-048-A — security and qualification gaps

Baseline: `4d0ce1e32c8dedde4e3a930abc2becf1f56b864e`.
Review date: 12 November 2026 (Asia/Kolkata).

This is a source/evidence gap register, not a claim that all vulnerabilities
were discovered or reproduced. No security predicate or production behavior
was changed. A historical successful signature check proves the signed bytes
under its stated anchor; it does not prove a truthful compromised sensor,
logical-agent attribution, complete capture or independent administration.

## Trust boundaries to preserve

The workload controls its own logs and application telemetry. The probe
measures supported host facts but still depends on the observed kernel. The
collector authenticates transport and signs the supplied observation stream
under its configured policy; cryptography does not make a compromised host
truthful. The independent comparator uses a different mechanism but may share
the same administrative failure domain. The verifier needs separately approved
trust material, coverage/window policy and completeness checkpoints. The AIP
logical agent, institution workload identity and observed process instance are
separate identifiers with explicit binding evidence.

No dependency installation, enforcement action, weakened identity/signature
predicate, manufactured benchmark or certification claim is proposed by this
register. P0-048-B is the next single work item; later gates remain open.
