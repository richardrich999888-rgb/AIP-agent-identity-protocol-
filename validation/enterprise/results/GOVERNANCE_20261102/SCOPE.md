# Validation scope

The component appendix proves actual pinned OPA evaluation and real OpenTelemetry SDK/Collector transport, strict privacy controls and error handling. Component record timestamps and policy/peer inputs are controlled test data; they do not establish actual agent behavior, operating-system peer measurement, business outcomes or independent custody.

The genuine governance-enabled workflow uses timestamps captured by the real controller around its actual operations. Its original signed AIP evidence and separate controller/OPA/OTel records require their own retained validation. Traces and policy audit metadata do not become signed kernel evidence.
