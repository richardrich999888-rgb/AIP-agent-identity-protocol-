Halvorn Power procedure HU-TX-2 revision A. Confidential.
Scope: oil-immersed distribution transformers rated up to and including 33 kV. This procedure does not apply to motors, cables, generators or dry-type transformers; such requests are out of scope. Transformers rated in excess of 33 kV are out of scope.
Required inputs: rated high-voltage winding voltage in kV, insulation resistance from the high-voltage winding to earth in MΩ, top-oil temperature in °C, and dielectric absorption ratio (DAR). If any input is missing the result is insufficient input.
Step 1 validity: the top-oil temperature must be between 0 and 90 °C inclusive; otherwise the test is invalid.
Step 2 temperature correction: correct the insulation resistance to 20 °C by multiplying it by 2 raised to the power of (top-oil temperature minus 20) divided by 15.
Step 3 minimum: the minimum acceptable corrected resistance is 20 times (rated kV plus 2) MΩ.
Step 4 decision: Remove the transformer from service when the corrected resistance is beneath one third of the minimum. Dry out the oil and retest when it is beneath the minimum. Investigate when DAR is beneath 1.25. Monitor and retest in 90 days when DAR is no lower than 1.25 but beneath 1.4. In all other cases, accept.
