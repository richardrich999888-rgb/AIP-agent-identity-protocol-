halvorn-power|hu-bt-4|restricted|HU-BT-4 rev B Stationary battery cell float acceptance
Halvorn Power procedure HU-BT-4 revision B. Confidential.
Scope: vented lead-acid cells in substation and UPS battery banks. This procedure does not apply to lithium, nickel-cadmium or NiCd batteries; such requests are out of scope. A voltage in excess of 3 V is a block or string voltage, not a cell voltage, and is out of scope.
Required inputs: cell float voltage in V, electrolyte temperature in °C, and specific gravity (SG) of the electrolyte. If any input is missing the result is insufficient input.
Step 1 validity: the electrolyte temperature must be between 5 and 45 °C inclusive; otherwise the reading is invalid and must be repeated.
Step 2 temperature correction: correct the cell voltage to 25 °C by adding 0.003 V for every degree Celsius in excess of 25 and subtracting 0.003 V for every degree beneath 25.
Step 3 decision: Replace the cell when the corrected voltage is beneath 2.10 V. Equalize charge the bank when it is beneath 2.17 V. Reduce the float voltage (overcharge) when it is in excess of 2.30 V. Otherwise, if the specific gravity is beneath 1.200, equalize charge. In all other cases, accept.
